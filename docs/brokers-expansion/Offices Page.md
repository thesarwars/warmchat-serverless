Offices page should act as a brokerage-level performance dashboard where brokers can monitor multiple office locations, teams, agents, lead flow, and pipeline performance from one place.

Structure:

* Top KPI cards:  
  * total offices  
  * total agents  
  * active conversations  
  * appointments booked  
  * pipeline value  
* Main office table should show:  
  * office name/location  
  * teams  
  * agents  
  * active leads  
  * pipeline value  
  * conversion %  
  * response time  
* Clicking an office opens a persistent right sidebar with:  
  * office details  
  * office stats  
  * top teams  
  * AI insights  
  * recent activity  
  * performance trends  
  * quick actions  
* Add charts for:  
  * pipeline growth  
  * leads by office  
  * conversion trends  
  * response times  
* Add live activity:  
  * appointments booked  
  * lead transfers  
  * new replies  
  * AI recovered leads

**GOAL:** Make the Offices page feel like a real-time AI-powered multi-office brokerage command center where brokers can monitor performance across locations, manage teams and agents, track pipeline/conversions, and optimize operations from one dashboard.
