ManyChat → WarmChats Lead Intake & AI Follow-Up Flow

Goal:
Any lead captured through ManyChat should automatically enter WarmChats, receive an instant response, be qualified by AI, and be guided toward scheduling an appointment.

Lead Capture

When a user submits information inside ManyChat:

* Name
* Phone Number
* Email
* Lead Source
* Platform (Instagram, Facebook, Website, etc.)
* Any answers collected by ManyChat

Zapier should immediately create or update the lead inside WarmChats.

Lead Creation

When lead enters WarmChats:

* Create contact record
* Assign lead source = ManyChat
* Assign platform source
* Save all collected fields
* Set status = New Lead
* Trigger AI Intake Workflow

Step 1: Instant Response

Send within 30–60 seconds.

Example:

"Hi {{first_name}}, thanks for reaching out. This is {{agent_name}}'s AI Agent. I'd love to help. Are you looking to buy, sell, or both?"

Record response automatically.

Step 2: Qualification

AI should collect:

Buyer Flow:

* Desired area
* Price range
* Timeline
* Pre-approved? (Yes/No)
* Cash or financing
* Appointment interest

Seller Flow:

* Property address
* Timeline to sell
* Reason for selling
* Property condition
* Appointment interest

AI should automatically populate CRM fields as answers are collected.

Step 3: No Response Follow-Up

If no reply:

Follow-Up #1:
1 day later

Follow-Up #2:
3 days later

Follow-Up #3:
7 days later

Follow-Up #4:
14 days later

Follow-Up #5:
30 days later

Stop immediately if lead replies.

Step 4: AI Lead Scoring

AI should automatically update lead status:

Cold
Warm
Hot
Appointment Ready

Hot lead triggers:

* Wants to buy/sell soon
* Requests appointment
* Requests showing
* Requests call
* Mentions active timeline

Step 5: Appointment Booking

When AI detects appointment intent:

Examples:

* "Can we meet?"
* "I'd like to see the house."
* "Can you call me?"
* "I'm ready."

AI should:

* Ask for preferred day/time
* Collect availability
* Offer booking link if available
* Notify assigned agent immediately

Step 6: Agent Handoff

Create notification when:

* Lead requests appointment
* Lead requests showing
* Lead asks financing questions
* Lead asks commission questions
* Lead asks contract/legal questions
* Lead becomes Hot

Agent receives:

* Lead summary
* Qualification answers
* Conversation transcript
* Recommended next action

CRM Auto Updates

AI should continuously update:

* Buyer/Seller status
* Lead stage
* Timeline
* Area
* Budget
* Property address
* Appointment status
* Last activity
* Lead score

Goal Outcome

Lead enters through ManyChat → WarmChats responds instantly → AI qualifies automatically → AI follows up until response → AI books appointments → Agent only gets involved when a lead is ready or needs human assistance.
