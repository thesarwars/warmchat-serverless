The Reporting page shows how the agent’s business is performing.

It should track leads, conversations, appointments, pipeline, AI impact, and closed business.

## **Global Controls**

At the top:

* Date filter: Last 7 days, 14 days, 30 days, 3 months  
* Export button  
* Tabs for each report section

All numbers should update based on the selected date range.

---

## **Overview Tab**

Shows the main business snapshot.

Metrics:

* Pipeline Value  
* Hot Leads  
* Appointments  
* Closed Deals  
* Lead → Appointment %  
* Average Response Time  
* Urgent Actions

Also show AI Insights like:

* Hot leads not responding  
* Appointments needing confirmation  
* Seller leads appointment-ready

Purpose: tell the agent what is working and what needs attention.

---

## **Lead Sources Tab**

Tracks where leads come from.

Sources:

* Zillow  
* Meta Ads  
* Open House  
* Google PPC  
* Website  
* Referral  
* Manual Entry  
* Other

Each source should show:

* Leads  
* Appointments  
* Closed Deals  
* Conversion rate

Purpose: show which sources are producing the best leads.

---

## **Appointments Tab**

Tracks appointment performance.

Metrics:

* Total Appointments  
* Completed  
* Upcoming  
* No-Shows  
* Appointment → Deal %

Charts show:

* Appointments booked over time  
* Appointment types

Types:

* Property Showing  
* Buyer Consultation  
* Listing Appointment  
* Phone Call  
* Zoom Meeting

Also show upcoming appointments list.

---

## **Closed Deals Tab**

Tracks completed business.

Metrics:

* Closed Deals  
* Volume Closed  
* Estimated Commission  
* Average Deal Size

Also show recent closings with:

* Property address  
* City  
* Closed price

---

## **Messaging Tab**

Tracks SMS and email performance.

### **SMS Metrics**

* Delivered

* Opt-outs

### **Email Metrics**

* Sent  
* Delivered  
* Open Rate

Message types:

* AI Auto-Reply

## **Calling Tab**

Tracks call activity.

Should show:

## **Goals Tab**

Tracks agent goals.

Should show progress toward:

# **Main Goal**

Reporting should help agents understand:

1. Where leads are coming from  
2. How many leads turn into appointments  
3. How many appointments turn into deals  
4. How much pipeline and commission they have  
5. How much work AI is doing  
6. What actions need attention

### **Calling Page**

**Purpose:** Track all call activity and show how calls contribute to appointments and deals.

#### **KPIs**

* Total Calls  
* Total Talk Time  
* Connect Rate (% answered)  
* Calls → Appointment %  
* Voicemails Left  
* Average Call Duration

#### **Call Activity Chart**

Shows calls placed over time based on selected date range.

#### **Call Outcomes**

Break down all calls into:

* Connected  
* Voicemail  
* No Answer  
* Missed Inbound

#### **Agent Performance**

For teams:

* Calls made per agent  
* Talk time  
* Connect rate  
* Appointments generated  
* Deals generated

#### **AI Integration**

Track:

* Missed call text-backs sent  
* Appointments booked from missed calls  
* AI call follow-ups sent  
* Leads re-engaged after missed calls

#### **Goal**

Agents should instantly know:

* How much they are calling  
* Whether people are answering  
* Whether calls are producing appointments

---

### **Goals Page**

**Purpose:** Help agents stay on track toward income and production goals.

#### **Main KPIs**

* Commission Earned  
* Deals Closed  
* Upcoming Deals  
* Goal Progress %

#### **Individual Goals**

Each user can set:

* Income Goal  
* Deal Goal  
* Appointment Goal  
* Lead Goal  
* Pipeline Goal

Show:

* Current Progress  
* Amount Earned  
* Goal Amount  
* Status (On Track / Behind / At Risk)

#### **AI Goal Coach**

AI should continuously analyze progress and recommend next actions.

Examples:

**On Track**

* Keep current pace  
* Follow up with hot leads  
* Confirm upcoming appointments

**Behind Goal**

* Contact X warm leads  
* Book X consultations  
* Make X calls  
* Send X follow-ups

#### **Pipeline Toward Goal**

Show:

* Closed Revenue  
* Under Contract Revenue  
* Active Pipeline Revenue

This helps agents see:

**“If everything closes, am I likely to hit my goal?”**
