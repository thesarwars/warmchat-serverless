Outbound Automations and Inbound AI are separate systems.

Outbound Automation:

* Runs scheduled follow-up campaigns.  
* Example: Day 0 SMS → Day 2 Follow-Up → Day 5 Follow-Up → Day 10 Follow-Up.  
* If the lead replies at any point, the automation immediately stops.  
* This prevents the lead from receiving irrelevant scheduled messages after engaging.

Inbound AI:

* Starts automatically when a lead replies.  
* AI takes over the conversation and continues qualifying the lead.  
* AI can ask questions, gather information, update stages, and guide the lead toward an appointment.

AI Actions:

* Ask qualification questions.  
* Detect buyer or seller intent.  
* Update lead stage automatically.  
* Recommend or book appointments.  
* Create tasks for agents.  
* Notify agents when human involvement is needed.  
* Pause when waiting for a lead response.

Example Flow:

No Reply  
→ Outbound Automation Running

Lead Replies  
→ Outbound Automation Stops

Inbound AI Starts  
→ AI Qualifies Lead  
→ AI Updates Stage  
→ AI Books Appointment  
→ AI Creates Tasks If Needed  
→ AI Continues Conversation Until Appointment, Human Takeover, or Lead Becomes Inactive

Important:  
Leads should never become fully manual just because an automation stops. Only the scheduled campaign stops. The AI conversation engine continues working and deciding the next best action based on the conversation.

**AI BRAIN** **\- AI Core Objectives**

1. Respond instantly.  
2. Qualify every lead.  
3. Detect motivation.  
4. Detect timeline.  
5. Detect budget/price range.  
6. Detect buyer vs seller.  
7. Detect objections.  
8. Book appointments.  
9. Notify agent when needed.  
10. Never let a lead go cold.

## **Stage 1: New Lead → Qualify**

AI should immediately determine:

### **Buyers**

* Looking to buy?  
* Where?  
* Price range?  
* Bedrooms/bathrooms?  
* Timeline?  
* Pre-approved?

### **Sellers**

* Looking to sell?  
* Property address?  
* Timeline?  
* Reason for selling?  
* Already listed?  
* Expected price?

### **Renter Qualification**

* Looking to rent?  
* Desired area?  
* Monthly budget?  
* Bedrooms/bathrooms?  
* Move-in date?  
* Pets?  
* Have proof of income?

### **Investor Qualification**

* Looking to buy and hold or flip?  
* Target area?  
* Budget?  
* Cash or financing?  
* Property type?  
* Timeline?

AI should not ask 10 questions at once.

One question at a time.

## **Stage 3: Objection Handling**

AI should know common objections.

### **Buyer**

* Need to save more money.  
* Need better credit.  
* Waiting for rates.  
* Just browsing.  
* Need to sell first.

### **Seller**

* Want higher price.  
* Waiting for market.  
* Interviewing agents.  
* Need repairs.

AI does NOT argue.

AI acknowledges then moves forward.

Example:

Lead:  
 "We're waiting for rates."

AI:  
 "That makes sense. A lot of buyers are monitoring rates right now. If rates improved tomorrow, would you be looking within the next few months?"

Keeps qualification moving.

---

## **Stage 4: Appointment Detection**

AI should constantly look for booking signals.

Examples:

* "Can someone call me?"  
* "Can I see the home?"  
* "What are the next steps?"  
* "Can you send listings?"  
* "Can I get a valuation?"  
* "Are you available this week?"

When detected:

### **Appointment Intent \= High**

AI immediately transitions into booking mode.

---

## **Stage 5: Booking Mode**

AI objective changes.

No more qualification.

Book appointment.

Example:

"Joseph is available this week. What day works best for a quick call?"

or

"Would you prefer an in-person consultation or a phone call?"

---

## **Stage 6: Human Takeover Detection**

AI should instantly notify agent when:

### **Urgent**

* Wants phone call  
* Wants showing  
* Wants offer written  
* Wants CMA  
* Wants listing appointment

### **Sensitive**

* Legal questions  
* Contract questions  
* Negotiation questions  
* Commission disputes

### **AI Confidence Low**

* Doesn't understand request  
* Multiple failed responses

---

## **Stage 7: Deal Stage Support**

Once appointment is booked:

AI doesn't disappear.

It becomes an assistant.

### **Examples**

Appointment tomorrow:

* Confirm appointment.  
* Send reminder.  
* Notify agent.

Showing scheduled:

* Confirm attendance.  
* Follow up afterward.

Offer submitted:

* Ask if agent needs follow up reminders.

---

## **Stage 8: AI Task Creation**

AI should create tasks automatically.

Examples:

### **High Priority**

* Lead requested callback.  
* Lead requested showing.  
* Lead requested CMA.  
* Lead replied after 30 days.  
* Appointment confirmation needed.

### **Medium Priority**

* No response 7 days.  
* Buyer became pre-approved.  
* Seller timeline changed.

---

## **Stage 9: AI Recommendations**

For every lead:

### **AI Next Best Action**

"Call today. Asked about weekend showings."

## **MVP AI Brain**

Keep one simple rule:

**Every message must either:**

1. Qualify the lead.  
2. Move toward an appointment.  
3. Create a task.  
4. Escalate to the agent.

If a message doesn't do one of those four things, the AI is wasting the conversation.

That single rule will make WarmChats feel far smarter than most real estate CRMs.
