# **AI Settings Page Requirements**

This page is the AI’s brain. Everything entered here controls how the AI speaks, qualifies, nurtures, responds, and escalates leads.

The AI should use this information in every conversation automatically.

---

## **1\. Identity Tab**

This is the AI Knowledge Profile.

The AI learns:

* Agent name  
* Brokerage  
* Phone  
* Email  
* Website  
* Service areas  
* Property types  
* Languages  
* Years of experience  
* Average price point  
* Booking link  
* Specialties  
* Commission structure  
* Current listings  
* Past sales  
* Preferred lender  
* Preferred title company

### **How it works**

The AI should answer using this information instead of guessing.

Example:

Lead:  
 “What areas do you serve?”

AI:  
 “I primarily help buyers and sellers throughout Los Angeles and the San Fernando Valley.”

Everything entered here becomes AI memory.

---

## **2\. Communication Tab**

Controls the AI personality.

Settings include:

* Tone  
* Message length  
* Emoji usage  
* Humor level  
* Response timing

### **How it works**

Examples:

Warm & Professional:  
 Professional but conversational.

Luxury & White Glove:  
 High-end client experience.

Direct & Concise:  
 Short and straight to the point.

Response timing controls how long AI waits before replying.

Default:  
 30-second natural delay.

---

## **3\. Qualifications Tab**

Controls lead qualification.

Users create questions for:

* Buyers  
* Sellers  
* Investors  
* Renters

Examples:

Buyer:

* Budget  
* Timeline  
* Pre-approval  
* Area

Seller:

* Property address  
* Motivation  
* Timeline  
* Expected price

### **How it works**

AI asks these naturally throughout conversations.

AI saves answers directly into CRM fields.

AI should never ask all questions at once.

AI asks only questions relevant to the lead type.

---

## **4\. Behavior Tab**

Controls how the AI behaves.

### **Qualification Style**

* Aggressive Qualification  
* Balanced Qualification  
* Relationship First

### **Goals**

* Book Appointments  
* Schedule Calls  
* Collect Information  
* Nurture Long-Term  
* Seller Consultations

### **Follow-Up Persistence**

* Low  
* Medium  
* High  
* Very High

### **How it works**

These settings influence every conversation.

Example:

Balanced Qualification:  
 Build rapport first, then qualify.

Relationship First:  
 More nurturing before asking questions.

---

## **5\. Rules Tab**

Controls AI guardrails.

### **Always Mention**

Examples:

* Local market expertise  
* Free valuation  
* Lending resources

### **Never Say**

Examples:

* Guaranteed results  
* Guaranteed pricing  
* Lowest rates

### **Escalate To Agent**

Automatically hand off when:

* Wants appointment  
* Wants phone call  
* Wants showing  
* Wants to buy now  
* Wants to sell now  
* Wants to make offer  
* Lead upset  
* Financing questions  
* Legal questions  
* Contract questions

### **How it works**

AI follows these rules in every conversation.

---

## **6\. FAQ Tab**

Agent-approved answers.

Examples:

* First-time buyer questions  
* Down payment questions  
* Home value questions  
* Selling timeline questions  
* Service area questions

### **How it works**

When a lead asks a matching question:

AI instantly uses the saved answer.

This creates consistency and prevents hallucinations.

---

## **7\. Training Tab**

AI testing environment.

Agent can chat with the AI before activating it.

Examples:

* What areas do you cover?  
* What is your commission?  
* Are you available this week?

### **How it works**

AI uses:

* Identity settings  
* Communication settings  
* Qualification settings  
* Rules  
* FAQs

to generate responses.

This page allows users to test and verify AI behavior before going live.

---

# **Overall AI Flow**

1. Lead sends message.  
2. AI responds in \~30 seconds.  
3. AI uses Identity, FAQs, Rules, and Communication settings.  
4. AI qualifies the lead naturally.  
5. AI updates CRM fields automatically.  
6. AI determines intent (Hot, Warm, Cold, Appointment Ready).  
7. AI nurtures cold leads automatically.  
8. AI books appointments when possible.  
9. AI escalates urgent situations to the agent.  
10. Agent only steps in when human attention is needed.

The goal is for WarmChats AI to operate like a trained real estate assistant that responds, qualifies, nurtures, updates CRM records, books appointments, and only involves the agent when necessary.
