Main AI Goal

WarmChats AI should always focus on:

1. Get a response
2. Qualify the lead
3. Push toward appointment/call/showing
4. Alert agent when human takeover is needed
5. Keep nurturing if lead does not reply

How AI Should Work
Inbound Leads

When a new lead comes in:

AI responds instantly
AI asks qualifying questions
AI identifies buyer/seller intent
AI tries to book appointment/call/showing
If lead is serious, confused, wants a call, or asks something complex → notify agent by SMS/app alert
If lead does not respond → AI starts nurture sequence automatically
Outbound Leads

When agent starts campaign:

Agent chooses template or creates one
AI personalizes message
AI sends follow-up sequence
AI watches replies
AI qualifies interested leads
AI pushes for appointment
AI alerts agent when it’s time to jump in
AI Human Takeover Rules

AI should notify agent when lead says:

“Can you call me?”
“I want to see the home”
“I’m ready to buy/sell”
“Can we meet?”
“What’s your commission?”
“I need pricing help”
“I have a specific property question”
Anything legal, contract, financing, negotiation, or complex

AI can handle:

Basic buyer/seller questions
Follow-up reminders
Appointment nudges
Lead qualification
General market/service questions
Template-based objections
Nurture conversations

Dashboard Should Show AI Guidance

Add a section called:

AI Next Steps

Examples:

“3 hot leads need agent response”
“12 leads are being nurtured by AI”
“5 buyers are ready for appointment”
“AI recommends calling Maria today”
“John asked about showing availability”
“Seller lead has not replied in 14 days - AI restarted nurture”

Best Feature Name

Instead of just “AI Agent,” call it:

AI Copilot
or
AI Follow-Up Copilot

Because it sounds like it is helping the agent everywhere, not just chatting.

Positioning: WarmChats helps real estate teams convert more leads by responding instantly, following up automatically, nurturing cold leads, and telling agents exactly when to step in.
