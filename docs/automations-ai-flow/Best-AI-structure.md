Best structure:

1. AI Agent
Your main control center.
It helps write replies, summarize leads, suggest next steps, improve tone, and assist inside inbox.

2. Inbound AI
Handles leads that come to you.

Examples:
New lead → instant reply
Lead replies → qualify
Missed call → auto text
Website form → create lead + respond
Email/SMS reply → update stage
Booking intent → push appointment

3. Outbound AI
Handles messages you send out.

Examples:
Cold follow-up campaigns
Open house follow-up
Re-engagement campaigns
Seller nurture
Buyer nurture
Mass email/SMS sequences
Scheduled follow-ups
