Automation workflow should be simple and AI-driven for real estate agents.

### **Action Plans**

Simple and easy to use.

Examples:

* New lead -> send text  
* No reply -> reminder  
* Appointment booked -> notify agent  
* Open house lead -> sequence

WarmChats should keep:

* Simple trigger system  
* Human-readable flows  
* Templates

Avoid making users build complex Zapier-style logic

Flow structure:

Trigger -> Conditions -> AI Action -> Timing -> Goal

Example Workflow:

1. Trigger  
    What starts the automation.  
    Examples:  
* New lead created  
* Lead replies  
* Missed call  
* Open house sign-in  
* No response for X days  
* Appointment booked  
2. Conditions  
    Rules for who enters workflow.  
    Examples:  
* Buyer lead  
* Seller lead  
* Zillow source  
* Area \= Beverly Hills  
* No assigned agent  
* Lead inactive 3+ days  
3. AI Actions  
    What AI does automatically.  
    Examples:  
* Send instant reply  
* Ask qualification questions  
* Push appointment  
* Send reminder  
* Re-engage cold lead  
* Generate follow-up  
* Move lead stage  
* Notify agent  
4. Timing  
    When actions happen.  
    Examples:  
* instantly  
* 30 minutes later  
* next day at 10AM  
* 3 days later  
* after appointment ends  
5. Smart AI Logic  
    Workflow should adapt automatically.  
    Examples:  
* lead replies -> pause automation  
* lead books appointment -> switch to reminder workflow  
* no response -> AI rewrites message  
* hot lead detected -> notify agent  
* appointment completed -> send follow-up  
6. Goal  
    Every workflow should have a purpose:  
* get reply  
* qualify lead  
* book appointment  
* nurture lead  
* confirm appointment  
* re-engage inactive lead

Goal overall:  
 Automations should feel like an AI Agent handling follow-up, reminders, scheduling, and nurturing automatically so agents spend less time manually chasing leads.

### **Speed-to-Lead Automation**

Example:

* Zillow lead comes in  
* Instant text/email sent  
* Agent notified immediately  
* Call option appears instantly

This is VERY aligned with WarmChats.

But WarmChats can make this way stronger with:

* AI conversational qualification  
* Dynamic responses  
* Auto-booking  
* AI follow-up sequences

That's your biggest opportunity.
