5. Inbound AI

Inbound AI's job is:

Respond immediately
Understand intent
Fill missing data
Determine readiness
Book appointment
Escalate when needed

The AI should first inspect the lead profile.

If profile is mostly empty:

Qualify heavily
Collect missing fields
Build lead profile

If profile is mostly complete:

Don't ask questions the agent already knows
Focus on moving toward appointment

Example:

Lead Profile:

Buyer
$700k budget
Fresno
Pre-approved
Timeline 0-3 months

AI should NOT ask:

"Are you looking to buy or sell?"

AI should say:

"Great. Are you still looking in Fresno around the $700k range?"

Then move toward:

"Would it make sense to schedule a quick consultation this week?"

5. Outbound AI

Outbound AI is different.

Its job is:

Generate conversations
Re-engage database
Revive cold leads
Detect opportunities
Book appointments

Not every outbound lead needs qualification.

Example:

Past client.

AI shouldn't ask:

"Are you buying or selling?"

Instead:

"Just checking in. How's everything going with the home?"

The conversation determines the next path.
