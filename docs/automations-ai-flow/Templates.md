Templates in WarmChats should power the entire AI automation system.  
 The goal is to let agents automate follow-up, nurturing, appointment booking, reminders, and re-engagement without manually texting every lead.

How Templates Should Work:

1. Template Categories  
    Organize templates by workflow/use case:  
* Buyer Follow-Up  
* Seller Follow-Up  
* Open House  
* Appointment Push  
* Re-Engagement  
* Missed Call Text  
* Nurture  
* Appointment Reminder  
* Post-Showing Follow-Up

Templates are not just single messages - they can be full AI sequences.

2. Template Structure  
    Each template contains:  
* message content  
* timing  
* channel (SMS/email)  
* AI goal  
* trigger conditions

Example:  
 Buyer Follow-Up Template  
 Step 1:  
 "Hey {{first\_name}}, are you still looking in {{area}}?"  
 -> send instantly

Step 2:  
 "Just checking in - any homes catch your eye?"  
 -> send 1 day later if no reply

Step 3:  
 "Would you like to schedule a quick call or showing?"  
 -> send 3 days later

3. Automation Triggers  
    Templates should automatically activate based on events.

Examples:

* new lead created  
* lead replies  
* missed call  
* appointment booked  
* no response for X days  
* open house sign-in  
* lead goes cold  
* AI detects booking intent

This is what powers the automation engine.

AI \+ Templates Working Together  
 Templates should not feel static.

AI should:

* personalize messages  
* adjust tone  
* insert lead context  
* rewrite messages  
* choose best send time  
* recommend next template

Example:  
 Template says:  
 "Are you still looking?"

AI transforms it into:  
 "Hey Sarah, are you still looking around Beverly Hills or have your plans changed?"

Smart Automation Logic  
 Automation should pause/change based on lead behavior.

Examples:

* lead replies -> stop current sequence  
* lead books appointment -> switch to reminder workflow  
* lead inactive -> move to nurture  
* AI detects high intent -> suggest appointment push

This prevents robotic spam.

6. Inline Editing UX  
    Agents should:  
* select template  
* preview/edit AI version  
* activate automation

Everything should happen inside inbox/workflow screens, not complicated builders.

7. Template Goals  
    Every template should have a purpose:  
* get reply  
* qualify lead  
* book appointment  
* re-engage  
* nurture  
* confirm meeting  
* recover cold lead

AI should optimize around that goal.

Goal:  
 Templates should act as reusable AI-powered automation workflows that help agents save time, follow up consistently, personalize outreach automatically, and convert more leads into appointments.
