I would give users 3 levels of AI control:

1. Global AI Toggle (AI Agent Page)

This is the master switch.

AI Agent
🟢 AI Enabled

When OFF:

AI stops replying
AI stops follow-ups
AI stops suggestions
AI stops appointment booking

This should be at the top-right of the AI Agent page (like your current Pause button).

2. Individual AI Agent Toggles

Inside AI Agent:

Inbound AI

🟢 Enabled

Handles:

Instant replies
Qualification
Lead scoring
Appointment booking

Toggle ON/OFF

Outbound AI

🟢 Enabled

Handles:

Follow-up sequences
Re-engagement campaigns
Nurture campaigns
Automated outreach

Toggle ON/OFF

AI Copilot

🟢 Enabled

Handles:

Smart replies
Lead summaries
Tone improvements
Next-step recommendations

Toggle ON/OFF

3. Per Lead Toggle

Inside every lead profile:

AI Status

🟢 AI Active

or

⚪ AI Paused

Buttons:

Pause AI
Resume AI

This is important because agents will often say:

"I’m taking over this conversation."

Then AI stops for that lead only.
